Ugadi Celebrations 2026

April 10, 2026
5:00 PM – 10:00 PM
Singapore Expo Hall 5
42 spots remaining

Join us for the grandest Ugadi celebration in Singapore! Ugadi marks the Telugu New Year and is a time for renewal, joy, and togetherness.

This year's celebration features traditional puja, cultural performances including classical dance and music, a grand vegetarian dinner, and entertainment for the whole family.

What to expect:

  • Traditional Ugadi Puja and Panchangam Sravanam
  • Classical dance performances by local artists
  • Live Telugu music concert
  • Kids entertainment zone
  • Grand vegetarian dinner buffet
  • Lucky draw with exciting prizes

About Our Community

Singapore Telugu Samajam (STS) was established in 2008 to serve the Telugu-speaking community in Singapore. Over 18 years, we've grown to over 1,200 members and hosted hundreds of cultural, social, and sporting events.

Our mission is to preserve and promote Telugu language, culture, and traditions while fostering community bonds among Telugu-speaking residents and families in Singapore.

Accessibility Notes

Keyboard Navigation

All interactive elements (buttons, links, form fields, toggles) are keyboard-navigable via Tab, Enter, Escape. Focus indicators visible on all elements.

Color Contrast

Primary text on white background: 8.59:1 ratio (AAA). All interactive elements meet 4.5:1 minimum. Badges use both color and text labels.

Screen Reader Support

Semantic HTML (nav, main, aside, section). ARIA labels on icon-only buttons. Live regions for toast notifications and OTP timer.

Forms

All inputs have associated labels. Error messages linked via aria-describedby. Required fields marked with asterisk and aria-required. Inline validation with clear error descriptions.

Responsive

Tested at 375px (mobile), 768px (tablet), 1280px (desktop). Touch targets minimum 48x48px. No horizontal scroll at any breakpoint.

Motion

Respects prefers-reduced-motion. Animations are subtle and non-essential. No auto-playing content.
